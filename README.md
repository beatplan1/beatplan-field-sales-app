# Beatplan.app: field sales app you buy once and own, with full source code

**Ownable software, not SaaS.** Beatplan.app is a field sales and fleet management app for distributors and companies whose reps visit dealers, retailers and farmers. It is sold as a **one-time licence with the full source code** (Expo / React Native for iPhone and Android, a web console, and a Supabase Postgres database), so there are **no per-user fees** and adding reps costs nothing.

It has run the field team of a real fertilizer distributor every working day since November 2025.

- Website and pricing: https://beatplan.app
- Live demo, no sign-up: https://beatplan-demo.netlify.app
- What it does and doesn't do: https://beatplan.app/facts

> **This repository contains no source code.** It describes the product. The source code is delivered on purchase from [beatplan.app](https://beatplan.app).

## Who it is for

- **Distributors and FMCG / CPG companies** with 10 to 200+ field reps who want to own their sales app instead of paying a monthly fee per user.
- **Seed, fertilizer, crop protection and biologicals companies** whose territory managers call on dealers and growers.
- **Software agencies and developers** who want a white-label field sales app to deliver to clients.

## What it does

| Area | Included |
|---|---|
| Beat plans | Daily visit plans and routes set by team leads |
| Visits | GPS-verified, time-stamped check-ins with photos and notes; visit history per dealer |
| Orders | Dealer order booking through review, dispatch and payment |
| Collections | Cash, cheque, bank and online payments; slip photo required for non-cash; validated by Accounts |
| Dealers | Dealer directory with outstanding balances and slipping-dealer alerts |
| Farmers / customers | Directory with crops and acres recorded by season, linked to dealers |
| Events | Farmer meetings and field days with attendee registration |
| Targets | Targets and dashboards by rep, territory, region; director dashboard |
| Fleet | Vehicles, drivers, inspections with photo checklists, odometer and fuel logs, service alerts, expense claims |
| Offline | Visits, daily plans, payments and fleet entries saved with no signal and synced without duplicates (dealer orders need a connection) |
| Roles | Field rep, team lead, business lead, key account manager, admin, director, accounts, logistics |
| Configuration | Company name, currency, regions, role and activity names, second language |

## What it does not do

- Dealer orders cannot be taken offline yet.
- No automatic route optimisation, CRM deal pipeline, shelf or merchandising audits, warehouse inventory, or lead / property data.
- No support contract: setup is done by you with your own ChatGPT, Claude or Codex using the included guide.

## FAQ

**Can I buy a field sales app's source code once instead of paying per user?**
Yes. Beatplan.app is sold as full source code for a one-time licence from US$2,900.

**Is it open source?**
No. It is commercial, source-available on purchase. You get the complete code and can change it for your own company (or your clients, on the Commercial and Agency licences).

**Is it self-hosted?**
Yes. It runs on your own Supabase project, and you publish the apps under your own company name.

**Is there a demo?**
Yes: https://beatplan-demo.netlify.app (no sign-up).
